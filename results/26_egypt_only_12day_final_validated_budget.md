## Why this is a young-traveler trip
- long international transit
- Cairo→Luxor overnight sleeper
- local rail Luxor→Aswan
- pre-dawn Abu Simbel run
- multiple cities with one backpack / luggage
- 10–12 days with frequent movement

It is less physically extreme than Annapurna, but stronger in history/scale and still has the “move while sleeping, wake up in another ancient city” graduation-trip feeling.

## Safety
This route avoids Sinai and land-border shortcuts. Current regional security alerts still require re-check before booking, but it removes the current Jordan Level-2 veto from the itinerary.
